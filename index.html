FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our furnished apartments in Mississauga.

Is there a minimum stay requirement for furnished apartments in Mississauga?

No, there is no minimum stay requirement. We offer flexible rental terms at our furnished apartments in Mississauga, allowing you to stay for as long or as short as you need.

What is included in the rental price of your Mississauga furnished apartments?

Our all-inclusive rental price covers parking, high-speed internet (WiFi), unlimited use of building facilities (pool, gym, sauna, theatre, bowling), cable TV with IP-TV in all languages, all utilities, and fully furnished accommodation with equipped kitchens, bedding, towels, and linens.

Where are your furnished apartments located in Mississauga?

Our furnished apartments are located at 3888 and 3880 Duke of York Blvd in the Ovation towers, right next to Square One Shopping Centre in Mississauga, Ontario. This prime location offers walking distance access to shopping, public transit (MiWay and GO Transit), restaurants, banks, schools, medical clinics, and more.

What building amenities are available at your Mississauga furnished apartments?

Our building offers a swimming pool, gym and exercise room, jacuzzi and sauna, theatre, bowling and billiard room, BBQ stations, air hockey, table tennis, 24-hour concierge, underground parking, and free high-speed WiFi. Each suite includes a fully equipped kitchen, in-suite washer and dryer, smart TV with IP-TV, and all bedding and linens.

How much do furnished apartments in Mississauga cost per month?

Monthly rates start at $3,700 CAD for a one-bedroom suite, $4,000 for a two-bedroom, $4,300 for a two-bedroom plus den, and $6,500 for a three-bedroom suite. Summer rates (June through September) are slightly higher. All prices are all-inclusive with no hidden fees. For stays under 30 nights, please contact us for a custom quote.

Are your furnished apartments a good alternative to hotels and Airbnb in Mississauga?

Yes! Our furnished apartments provide more space, privacy, and value than hotels. With full kitchens, in-suite laundry, free parking, and all building amenities included, our guests consistently rate us 9.3/10 on Booking.com. Unlike Airbnb, we offer professional management with 24/7 on-site staff and transparent pricing with no hidden fees.
