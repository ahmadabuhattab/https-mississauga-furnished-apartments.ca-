Booking Details

Deposit, payment, and cancellation information for your stay.

Deposit required to reserve any suite

CDN$500

This deposit will be considered as a security deposit / damage deposit / key deposit upon check-in, and will be refunded to you upon check-out if there is no damage or missing keys.

Ways to pay the deposit

  • Cash or Interac debit
  • Cheque, money order, or bank draft
  • Visa, Mastercard, or American Express
  • Wire transfer
  • Email money transfer
  • Western Union
  • Direct deposit

For a wire transfer, we’ll send the bank account information directly to you.

Cancellation or changes

Under 15 days Bookings made or changed within this window are final. Cancelling or changing the stay means the CDN$500 deposit is forfeited.
15+ days A CDN$50 administration fee applies. The remaining deposit balance is refunded.
